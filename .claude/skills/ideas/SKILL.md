Ideas the human brought, if any: $ARGUMENTS

Generate real candidates, score them honestly, and kill the bad ones — including the human's own. They can come up with horrible plans and have asked to be told. This runs once, at kickoff, on the bigger model: 25 minutes, at most 3 AskUserQuestion calls. This skill and `/spec` are exempt from the one-question-per-turn and show-something rules (CLAUDE.md → How to read me): the cards and the score table are what the human sees.

0. SPEC.md exists, or PHASE ≥ 2 → change nothing: "/ideas is kickoff-only — the idea is already chosen (PHASE n)." CLAUDE.md → Idea already filled (a second run) → it is a candidate too, and the `RUNNER-UP` line gets replaced, not appended.
1. PHASE 0 → do PLAYBOOK Phase 0 first (5 minutes). Read CLAUDE.md → "Sponsor / company challenges to target" and Stack, and the "what wins here" paragraph in `.claude/skills/spec/SKILL.md` — it is the lens for everything below. Sponsor list still a placeholder → ask for it; if the human says it isn't out yet, write `not released yet (HH:MM)` under the heading, score sponsor fit as unknown, and re-check when it's pasted.
2. **Interview: one AskUserQuestion, 4 questions at most.** If the Timeline still has placeholders, K and E (PLAYBOOK → Knowing where we are) take the first two slots and only Q1 and Q2 follow. No recommendation and no leading first option on questions about the human — a tired person clicks the first thing.
   - Q1, the signal: "What do you do all day — job, classes, hobby — and what annoyed you or someone near you this month?" Options are the theme tracks as examples only; say the real answer goes in Other.
   - Q2, multi-select facts: first hackathon · student · phone camera at the table · can print a prop · own sample data or photos for something.
   - Q3, what they'd rather demo, as what the judge sees: a photo goes in and a verdict or transformation comes out · the screen updates live as the judge acts · a before/after · a map, timeline, or chart fills in.
   - Q4, only if there is room: the one thing they refuse to build.
3. **Generate 8–10 one-line candidates internally, then cull to 4 cards** (≤ 80 words each) plus one line each for the rest under "also considered — cut because …". Ideas the human brought are extra cards, scored like the rest; they don't take a slot. Rules:
   - Build only from what the stack does cheaply: per-user state, photo/file upload, Gemini text, JSON, and vision, a deployed URL, a phone-friendly page. Nothing that needs hardware, scraping, a paid API, real people's private data, third-party approval, visiting untrusted URLs, or a dataset you can't produce in an hour.
   - Before writing a card, name the project you'd expect in any hackathon gallery for that problem. If your pitch sentence describes that project too, add a specific twist or drop the candidate.
   - The technical piece must be something a chat window can't do: a decode, a measurement, a cross-check, a real-time loop, a transformation. "Gemini looks at it and explains" alone is out.
   - The demo is 3 minutes, one judge, at the table, on the laptop plus whatever Q2 said the human has. The judge moment is watched, within about 10 seconds of the judge's own action, never explained.
   - Sponsor fit: a theme track fits only if you can write "fits X because the judge watches Y" — otherwise it is forced and not claimed. API tracks: at most one, only if it is the heart of the idea and its free key is obtainable in 10 minutes at the event; `/spec`'s sponsor rule governs extras. Aim for two strong fits if the list allows it without forcing; if it doesn't, say so.
   Card format: **Name** — one-sentence pitch · For: … · Judge watches: … · Hard part: … · Sponsors: … (one "because" each) · Skeleton by K+10 (≈ 9 hours of building): yes / risky — what sinks it.
4. **Score every card on the five judging criteria, 1–5.** Anchors: 5 = the best project in the gallery on this criterion, 3 = typical, 1 = would hurt. Completion: the demo path is buildable by K+10 by one person. Originality: leave blank for now. Design: the wow moment is watched, not explained. Technology: the code does something beyond one model call — that alone is a 2. Practicality: a named real person could use it tomorrow. A card at 1–2 on Completion is out regardless of total.
5. **Originality search, at most 5 minutes, for the top 3 by the other four scores:** Devpost galleries (ShellHacks and similar hackathons) and the obvious existing products, 2–3 searches each. Fill in Originality: 1 = a direct match exists, 5 = no match for the twist. A card whose twist already exists is out. Say what exists and what is different.
6. **Table and verdict:** all cards with totals; sponsor fit breaks ties, then Originality. Recommend one, with its single biggest risk and the cheapest mitigation (a backup photo in `backend/demo/`, a manual fallback path). One line each on what is out and why.
7. **Pick — one AskUserQuestion:** the recommended card first; the runner-up (the best card that isn't out — say if that differs from the score order); combine two, only if the combination beats both and you say how; regenerate, with what to change — once, then the human picks, and "you pick" means the top score. In the question text, ask them to check one thing: would they enjoy pitching this ten times? If not, it's the wrong one.
8. Write CLAUDE.md → Idea (one paragraph: what it does, who it's for, the judge moment, the sponsor tracks claimed) and append `RUNNER-UP (not built): …` to Decisions — PLAYBOOK Phase 2 says when to switch to it. Don't commit; `/spec` does. Then say: "type `/spec <the idea in one sentence>` — same model, no `/clear`."
